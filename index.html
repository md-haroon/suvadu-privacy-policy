SUVADU

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to SUVADU ("the App"). SUVADU is an expense tracking application designed to help users record and manage their personal and business expenses.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SUVADU.

2. Information You Enter

SUVADU allows you to enter information related to your expenses, including:

  • Expense titles
  • Expense amounts
  • Categories and subcategories
  • Dates and times
  • Payment methods
  • Notes or descriptions
  • Profile information created within the App
  • Budget information

This information is used to provide the expense tracking functionality of the App.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your expense and profile information is stored locally on your device using the App's local database and storage mechanisms.

SUVADU does not require you to create an online account to use the core expense tracking features.

Data stored locally on your device remains under your control. If the App is uninstalled or its local data is cleared, locally stored information may be permanently deleted.

4. Data Collection

SUVADU does not intentionally collect or sell your personal expense information to third parties.

The App does not use your expense information for advertising or profiling purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties.

Information you enter into SUVADU is not intentionally shared with third parties through the core functionality of the App.

6. Financial Information

SUVADU is an expense tracking tool. It does not provide banking, lending, investment, payment processing, or financial advisory services.

You should avoid entering highly sensitive information such as passwords, PINs, banking credentials, or card security codes into expense notes or other fields.

7. Permissions

SUVADU only requests permissions that are necessary for features provided by the App.

If future versions introduce additional features requiring new permissions, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. Children's Privacy

SUVADU is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and any device-level authentication used to protect access to their data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or SUVADU, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing for the App.
